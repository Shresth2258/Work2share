Questions, answered.

Everything people ask us about coworking, virtual offices and meeting rooms.

Where is Work2Share?

Plot 42, Udyog Vihar, Sector 18, Sarhol, Gurugram, Haryana 122015. You can find the map in the footer of every page.

Who can use Work2Share?

Freelancers, startups, growing companies and business owners. If you need a desk, a business address or a meeting room, we have a plan for you.

Is access available round the clock?

Members get 24/7 access. For reception and visitor timings, message us on WhatsApp.

What coworking options do you have?

Hot desks, dedicated desks and private cabins.

Can I pay by the day, month or year?

Yes. You can pick a plan by the day, month or year.

What facilities are included?

Fast Wi-Fi, a pantry, power backup, security and housekeeping, plus our community. Ask us for the details of each plan.

What is a virtual office?

It gives your business a professional address, mail handling and access to meeting rooms, without a physical desk or lease.

Can I use the address for my business?

Yes, that’s what it’s for. Message us and we’ll explain what is needed for your business.

Can I book a meeting or conference room for a few hours?

Yes. Both our meeting room and our conference room are booked by the hour.

What is the difference between the meeting room and the conference room?

The meeting room suits small groups, client calls and interviews. The conference room is for larger groups, presentations and workshops. Share your headcount and we will suggest the right one.

How do I check room availability?

Message us on WhatsApp with your date, time and group size and we’ll confirm.

How do I get a quote?

Tell us what you need on WhatsApp and we’ll send you a clear quote.

Can you build a plan around my team?

Yes. Tell us your team size and needs and we’ll suggest a plan that fits.
