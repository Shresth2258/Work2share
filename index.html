Ask on WhatsApp
What is the difference between coworking and a virtual office?

Coworking gives you a desk or cabin to work from. A virtual office gives you a professional business address and mail handling, without a physical workspace.

Can I book a meeting or conference room for just a few hours?

Yes. Both our meeting room and our conference room are available by the hour, so you pay only for the time you use.

What is the difference between the meeting room and the conference room?

The meeting room suits small groups, client calls, interviews and team catch-ups. The conference room is for larger groups, presentations, workshops and trainings. Tell us how many people are coming and we will suggest the right one.

How do I book a tour?

Message us on WhatsApp at +91 83839 63820 or call +91 93151 31014. We will arrange a visit at a time that suits you.

Where is Work2Share located?

Plot 42, Udyog Vihar, Sector 18, Sarhol, Gurugram, Haryana 122015. You can find the map and directions on our Contact page.

Can you build a plan around my team?

Absolutely. Tell us your team size and needs, and we will suggest a plan that fits.
